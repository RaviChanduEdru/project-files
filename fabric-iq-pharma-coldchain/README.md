# Aurora Pharma — cold-chain demo data

The dataset for the two-part series **[Build a live pharma cold-chain monitor in Microsoft Fabric Real-Time Intelligence](https://ravichanduedru.me/articles/fabric-iq/build-pharma-coldchain-fabric-rti)** (Part 1) and **[the ontology and the agents](https://ravichanduedru.me/articles/fabric-iq/build-pharma-coldchain-fabric-iq)** (Part 2).

Aurora Pharma is a fictional manufacturer in Hyderabad shipping insulin and vaccines across twelve sites in refrigerated trucks. Eight CSV files hold the whole operation. Two incidents are baked into the data on purpose: batch **AUR-2207**, frozen solid for its entire journey on a truck that never once got warm, and batch **AUR-2211**, which quietly used 105 of its 120 excursion minutes across three different trucks. Finding both automatically is what the series builds.

**[Download all eight as a ZIP](https://github.com/RaviChanduEdru/project-files/raw/main/fabric-iq-pharma-coldchain/fabric-iq-pharma-coldchain.zip)**, or grab individual files below.

## How the tables connect

- A **shipment** travels as a chain of **legs**. Each leg is one truck, one stretch of road, one time window.
- A **batch** of medicine belongs to a **product** and rides a shipment. A batch can change trucks between legs, and that handover is where problems hide.
- **`leg_cargo`** is the shipment plan flattened for joining: one row per leg per batch, carrying that product's safe temperature band (`MinSafeC` to `MaxSafeC`) and its whole-journey excursion budget (`BudgetMinutes`).
- **`reefer_telemetry_seed`** is ten days of recorded truck temperatures, one reading per truck per 15 minutes. A reefer is a refrigerated truck.

## Notes on the data

- In Part 1 of the series, only the first six files become lakehouse tables. `leg_cargo.csv` loads into the Eventhouse, and `reefer_telemetry_seed.csv` is replayed through an Eventstream so sensor data has exactly one path in.
- `IntervalMinutes` says how many minutes each reading represents (`15.0` in the seed, `0.5` when the article's live stream runs). The KQL in the article reads this field instead of assuming a cadence, so keep the column when sending events.
- `FreezeAllowed` is `false` for every chilled product. Freezing has no budget: one frozen reading is an instant quality failure.
- All numbers are generated by script and cross-checked by a verification script; nothing is hand-typed.
